# Reports & Analytics — Phase 10 Forecast Reports

## Delivered

- Three-month shopping recorded-spend trend from up to six completed, contiguous months with recorded purchases.
- Current-month spending pace using a straight daily run rate after at least three elapsed days and one current-month purchase.
- Three-month instrument price/NAV trend scenarios when at least three completed, contiguous monthly observations are available.
- Existing unpaid subscription entries due in the next 90 days, shown as scheduled records rather than inferred recurring costs.
- A manually configured savings goal with a straight-line months-to-target estimate, saved in preferences.

## Assumptions and exclusions

- Missing shopping months block the historical trend; no missing month is treated as zero. Purchase records have no per-record currency, so spend values are not converted or assigned a currency.
- Budget pace assumes the recorded daily run rate continues through month end; it is not a commitment or complete-spending estimate.
- Investment scenarios project per-unit recorded price/NAV only. They do not predict portfolio returns, include contributions, or provide advice.
- Due entries are existing unpaid records only; no future subscription payment is generated from billing type.
- Savings-goal projections use the entered current amount and monthly contribution only; no interest, investment return, or inflation is assumed.
